Why, Drawn

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the Why, Drawn uploader: a personal tool the owner of the Why, Drawn YouTube channel uses to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the uploader asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to post the channel's videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel it is posting to, and to read the privacy, processing state and view counts of the videos it uploaded.

It does not read, change or delete anything else in the Google account or the YouTube channel.

How data is stored and shared

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a local file that is never uploaded or shared. The uploader has no server and keeps no database. It does not collect data about viewers, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file on the owner's computer also ends it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Why, Drawn YouTube channel.
