Why, Drawn

Anything, explained in 30-second drawings.

Why, Drawn is a YouTube channel of short animated explainers: science, money, history and how things work, with real numbers drawn out so they make sense. A new short every day.

About the uploader

The channel's videos are made on the channel owner's own computer and uploaded to YouTube with a small personal tool that uses the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner, to post the channel's own videos. Nobody else can sign in to it.
